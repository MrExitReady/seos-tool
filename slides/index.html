FAQ

Where do my notes go?

Nowhere. NoteDeck runs entirely in your browser — there is no server, no account and no upload. Close the tab and your notes stay on your device only.

How do I get a PDF?

Click Export PDF and choose “Save as PDF” in the print dialog. Each slide becomes one full landscape page.

How does the Pro license work?

After purchase you receive a license key by email. Click “Enter license key”, paste it once, and Pro is unlocked in that browser permanently. No subscription, no expiry.

Can I use it offline?

Yes — once the page has loaded, everything works without a connection.
