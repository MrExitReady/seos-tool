## Posting strategy (keep it simple)

- **Facebook + YouTube are your priority** — that's where 50–70 year old
  owners actually are. TikTok/Instagram are bonus reach.
- Post the feed content once now, then re-post a variant monthly. Evergreen
  tools can be promoted forever — nobody remembers last month's post.
- Every podcast episode: mention the calculator once, verbally —
  "run your numbers at exit dot fisherandfisher dot au" — and keep the
  YouTube description block in every upload.
- Watch GHL: leads arriving with `utm_source=facebook` etc. tell you which
  platform deserves more effort. Double down on whichever converts to
  scorecard completions and booked calls — not raw clicks.
