My 6-Week Challenge

A personal weight-loss plan and reference — written for me: female, 59, 165 cm, starting weight 80 kg, office-based, in menopause. Private, personal use only.

The goal: lose as much fat as safely possible in 6 weeks. The challenge headline is 9 kg; my realistic, safe target with excellent adherence is 5–7 kg (week 1 usually drops an extra 1–2 kg of water weight, which helps the total). Anything at or above 6 kg is an outstanding result at my age and starting point.

How the maths works: eating ~1,300 kcal against a maintenance of ~1,650 kcal creates a food deficit of ~350 kcal/day. Daily walking and strength work add another ~250–350 kcal. That's ~600–700 kcal/day, or roughly 0.6–0.8 kg of fat per week, compounding over 6 weeks.

Before starting: confirm with my GP that this plan is right for me (menopause, any medications, blood pressure). Never eat below 1,200 kcal/day — under-eating stalls results, burns muscle and worsens menopause symptoms.

2 · Daily Non-Negotiables

  1. Calories: 1,250–1,300 kcal/day (≈5,300 kJ). Log everything — every bite, lick and taste.
  2. Protein at every meal: 25–35 g per main meal to reach 95–100 g/day. Protein protects muscle and keeps me full.
  3. Steps: minimum 8,000/day weeks 1–2, then 10,000/day. Break up desk time — 3–4 minutes of walking every hour.
  4. Water: 2 litres (8 cups) minimum. One cup before each meal.
  5. Zero alcohol for the full 6 weeks. No sugary drinks, no fried food, no desserts except the planned snacks.
  6. Kitchen closes 3 hours before bed. Helps sleep, reflux and hot flushes.
  7. Sleep: 7–8 hours. Poor sleep raises hunger hormones the next day.
  8. Weigh-in: once a week, same day (Saturday), same time (first thing, after the bathroom, before breakfast), same scales. Record it in the tracker.
  9. Strength training: 3 sessions per week, non-negotiable — it's what protects bone and muscle during menopause.
Slip-up rule: one off-plan meal never ruins a week — the response does. Never "write off the day". The next meal goes straight back on plan.

3 · The 6-Week Roadmap — Step by Step

Week 0 (Prep — do this before Day 1)

  • GP check-in; confirm the plan is safe for me.
  • Take starting measurements: weight, waist (at navel), hips (widest point), and a front/side photo in fitted clothes.
  • Pantry clean-out: remove or box up biscuits, chips, chocolate, soft drink, alcohol.
  • Do the Week 1 shop from the shopping list below. Meal-prep Sunday: cook 2 batches (soup + a protein), boil 4 eggs, chop snack veg.
  • Set phone reminders: hourly desk-break, water, 9pm kitchen-closed.

Week 1 — Foundation

  • Follow the meal plan exactly; weigh/measure every portion this week to calibrate my eye.
  • Walk 30 minutes every day (split into 2×15 if easier). Target 8,000 steps.
  • 2 strength sessions (Workout A below).
  • Expect the biggest scale drop of the challenge (1.5–2.5 kg, mostly water — take it, but don't expect it to repeat).

Week 2 — Build

  • Move to 3 strength sessions (A, B, A).
  • Walks go to 40 minutes; steps to 9,000.
  • Batch-cook twice this week so there's always a compliant meal ready.

Week 3 — Halfway Push

  • Mid-challenge measurements: weight, waist, hips, photos. Waist and hips often move even when the scale pauses.
  • Add intervals to 2 walks: 1 minute brisk / 2 minutes easy, repeated 8 times.
  • Steps to 10,000/day from here on.

Week 4 — Plateau-Proofing

  • If the scale hasn't moved in 10+ days: re-weigh portions for 3 days (portion creep is the usual culprit), check sauces/oils/milky coffees, and keep going — measurements count too.
  • Swap in the alternate meals (swap list below) to stop boredom.
  • Strength: add a rep to every set, or a heavier bottle/weight.

Week 5 — Peak Week

  • 10,000+ steps every single day; one longer weekend walk of 60–75 minutes.
  • 3 strength sessions (B, A, B).
  • Tighten the easiest leaks: no untracked extras, water before every meal.

Week 6 — Finish Strong

  • Run the week exactly like Week 5 — no crash tactics, no skipping meals before the final weigh-in.
  • Final weigh-in and measurements Saturday morning; final photos.
  • Plan the week after: lift calories to ~1,500–1,600 kcal (maintenance-minus), keep the walks, keep strength training, keep the weekly weigh-in. This locks the result in.

Swap list (like-for-like, any day)

  • Proteins: chicken breast ⇄ turkey breast ⇄ firm tofu (same weight ×1.2) ⇄ white fish; salmon ⇄ tinned salmon 95 g; beef mince ⇄ kangaroo or extra-lean pork mince.
  • Carbs (½ cup cooked): brown rice ⇄ quinoa ⇄ wholemeal couscous ⇄ 150 g sweet potato ⇄ ½ cup corn.
  • Breakfasts, lunches and dinners can swap between days freely — the daily total is what matters.
  • Eating out rule: grilled protein + salad/veg, dressing on the side, water to drink. Skip the bread basket and chips.
Portion cheat-sheet: 100 g raw chicken ≈ palm of my hand · ½ cup cooked rice ≈ a cupped handful · 1 tsp oil ≈ a fingertip · 20 g nuts ≈ one small handful.

Strength A (20 min, home, no equipment needed)

2 rounds weeks 1–2, 3 rounds from week 3. Rest 60–90 seconds between rounds.

  • Sit-to-stand from a chair — 12 reps (progress: don't touch down between reps)
  • Wall push-ups — 12 reps (progress: hands on kitchen bench, then knees on floor)
  • Glute bridges on the floor — 15 reps
  • Bent-over row with 2 filled water bottles or shopping bags — 12 reps
  • Standing calf raises holding the bench — 15 reps
  • Plank on knees — hold 20–40 seconds

Strength B (20 min)

  • Step-ups onto the bottom stair — 10 each leg
  • Shoulder press with water bottles — 12 reps
  • Reverse lunges holding the bench for balance — 8 each leg
  • Farmer's carry: heaviest comfortable bags, walk 30–40 m — 3 carries
  • Bird-dog (opposite arm/leg reach on all fours) — 8 each side
  • Dead bug — 10 each side

Office-day movement

  • Every hour: stand and walk 3–4 minutes (set a reminder). This alone adds ~2,500 steps.
  • Walk 15 minutes at lunch, every workday, rain plan included (shopping centre or stairs).
  • Take stairs over lifts; park further away; walking meetings for phone calls.
Safety: strength work should feel challenging, never painful in a joint. Any chest pain, dizziness or unusual breathlessness = stop and see the GP.

7 · Menopause-Specific Notes

  • Protein is priority one. Menopause accelerates muscle loss; 95–100 g/day plus strength training is what prevents "losing weight but feeling weaker".
  • Bone density: aim for ~1,300 mg calcium daily — this plan's dairy (yoghurt, milk, cottage cheese) plus greens gets close; discuss a supplement with the GP if dairy drops.
  • Belly-first weight is hormonal, not failure. Post-menopause fat stores shift to the middle. The waist measurement will respond — track it as seriously as the scale.
  • Hot flushes: alcohol, caffeine after midday, spicy food and poor sleep are the common triggers — this plan already removes two of them.
  • Sleep before willpower: a short night measurably increases next-day hunger. Protect the 7–8 hours like an appointment.
  • Expect slower, not impossible. Loss may run 10–20% slower than a younger woman's — the plan already accounts for this. Consistency beats intensity every week of this challenge.
  • On HRT or other medications? Flag the calorie target with the GP; some medications affect appetite or fluid retention and the weigh-in trend.
